0.11.0-screen · the locked mix

Tiago locked this layout. It keeps the live top bar, the D1 rocket sheet and a cleaner Land and Inspector. The rules stay the same. Only the screen and the part names change.

  • Top bar. Range lamp, sky and wind on the left. Money, Steel, RP-1, LOX, Fleet centered as one gold inline row (D3), version under. Day and a small Pause chip on the far right. No buy buttons in the top bar. Steel buys stay on Land. RP-1 and LOX loads stay pad actions.
  • Buys stay where they are. Steel buy cards stay on Land. RP-1 and LOX loads are pad actions in the Inspector.
  • Left is Land. Owned buildings, such as Starfactory, and free plots to buy. Buildings stay on the left.
  • Right is the Inspector. It shows the selected building only. One line of description, then data with compact rocket slots, then an Upgrade section inside the scroll (Another slot, Pad level 2, the launch farm tanks). Raise Merlin and Lighten the stage stay Starfactory jobs, started from the rocket sheet. Only do-now actions (Build, Roll, Static fire, Load fuel) sit in the pinned Actions strip at the bottom. The fleet lives in the Fleet sheet.
  • Rocket sheet. Three parts: Structure, Fuel lines, Engine. Each part shows its %. The big % is the lowest part. Fitting and Hull are gone.
