A 10 km hop, and the next article while you test

This row is the high hop. The two light rules from the dropped pipeline row ride here. The factory may start the next article once the first has left the hold. The header shows the fire count the pad already has. The numbers below are initial balance. A later sitting can move them.

SN 1 is ready on the mount. You pick 10 km. The climb is 12 seconds, the belly is 8, the flip is 3, and the landing burn is 6. The engine is off for the belly and the flip. The landing floor is 1535 kN. The flight mass is 107883 kg.

What you see

A new build is named SN 1, SN 2, and so on. A Starhopper already in the save keeps that name. It keeps 25 m, 50 m, and 150 m. It does not get the 10 km chip.

SN 1 keeps those three hops and adds one chip, 10 km. You finish the same cryo proof, tanking run, static fire, and 1 day data review. Then you pick the height and press Hop. The tanks load. The day holds. The camera locks a frame about 800 m wide.

The body climbs nose up, turns onto its belly, flips back, and lands on the same mark the low hop uses, about 100 m east of the mount. A soft landing rolls home and takes the same 1 day inspection. A hard landing names the short part. A scrub spends nothing.

While SN 1 is on the pad, the Starfactory can build SN 2. The factory hold stays one. Mega Bay still keeps a slot open for the rocket that has to come home. The header shows Fires and the same count the pad calls Fires, last 30 days.

The look

These three pictures are the whole screen at 1280 by 800. The first is the ready pad with 10 km picked. The second is the belly, engine off. The third is the factory building the next article while the first is in test. The type outside the top bar stays 11, 12, 14, or 16 px.

The name

The article on the roadmap is still one Raptor vehicle. Its screen name for a new build becomes SN. The first new build is SN 1. The count is how many live vehicles already use that SN name. A stored Starhopper does not take a number.

An old save that says Starhopper 1 still says Starhopper 1. The low hop chips stay on it. The 10 km chip is absent. The factory card says Builds an SN.

SH stays free for Super Heavy at 0.20.0-stack. This row does not write SH.

The flight

The 10 km hop uses the same propellant formula as the low hop. Dry mass stays 102000 kg. Specific impulse stays 330 seconds. The mix stays 3.6 kg of liquid oxygen for each kg of methane. Lit time is 18 seconds. That is the climb plus the landing burn.

The load is 1279 kg of methane and 4604 kg of oxygen. The flight mass is 107883 kg. The landing floor is 1535 kN. That is flight mass times 9.81 times 1.45, rounded, the same floor rule as the low hop. Structure Needs stays 70. Fuel lines Needs is 18 seconds, the lit time.

The engine seconds and the fuel-line seconds fall only while the engine is on. The belly and the flip spend none. A low hop still spends its whole burn, because that burn never turns the engine off in the middle.

The landing check runs at the start of the landing burn. It reads thrust against 1535 kN and fuel lines against the seconds still needed. A short names the part. A hard landing cuts 40 off Structure, leaves it at 20 at worst, clears the cryo proof and the tanking run, and the rocket needs Mega Bay. A soft landing keeps the tests. A soft 10 km landing pays Land a hop once, if that goal is still open. The low hop already pays that goal. This row does not add a second goal.

A closed range or a short tank scrubs before the fill. Nothing is spent. Skip during the count, the climb, the belly, the flip, or the landing burn does not jump. The log says the rocket is still in flight. The day holds for the whole watch.

The frame is about 800 m, centered on the mount and the landing mark. The height number is the 10 km read. The picture does not zoom out to 10 km. The low hop frame stays 400 m.

The factory

The factory hold is still one finished article. You cannot start a build while a finished article sits there. Once that article has left, you can start the next build while it is in the bay or on the pad. startBuildRocket already works this way. This row does not add a second hold. It keeps the path, and the factory card shows the build while the pad card shows the article in test.

The day hold still stops the factory countdown during a fill, a fire, a low hop, and the 10 km hop. The build resumes from the same days left when the hold ends.

returnClaim stays. A rocket on the pad, or on the way to the pad, still owns a bay slot. The log stays "Leave a bay slot open. A rocket outside the bay still has to come home." A second slot, bought for 30 steel, is the way to house both.

The header

The header gains one counter, Fires, in the same row as Money, Steel, CH4, LOX, and Fleet. The number is fireDays whose day is inside the last 30 days. That is the same count as the pad row Fires, last 30 days. A static fire that lights joins it, as it does today. A hop does not join it. A 10 km hop does not join it. A scrub does not join it.

  • Dry mass stays 102000 kg. Steel for a build stays 28. The build stays 8 to 12 days.
  • The 25 m, 50 m, and 150 m profiles stay. Their frames stay 400 m.

  • The review still pays 12 money for each clean test every 30 days. A soft hop is still a clean test.
  • Save version stays 1. There is no sound.

Real life

Starhopper flew 150 m on 27 August 2019 with one Raptor. SN5 and SN6 flew the same height in August 2020. SN8 flew 12.5 km on 9 December 2020 and was lost on the landing. SN9, SN10, and SN15 flew 10 km. SN15 landed on 5 May 2021. Those flights used three Raptors, a header tank, and a flip with the engines lit.

This row keeps one Raptor and the dry mass you already have. The 10 km read is the height those later flights used. The watch is compressed, the same way the 150 m hop is a 22 second burn. The engine stays off through the flip so the landing burn is the check on the parts table. A later sitting can light the flip.

The save

Save version stays 1. The label is 0.19.0-high-hop. A vehicle with no stored name pattern loads as it does today. hopMeters may be 25, 50, 150, or 10000. Any other stored height loads as null. fireDays loads as it does today. A hop in the middle of the watch does not save. A reload shows the rocket ready on the mount, and the tanks are not drained. That is the low hop rule, and this row uses it.

Test hooks

window.__sb keeps the hooks it has. startHop(name, meters) accepts 10000 for an SN. It returns false for 10000 on a Starhopper. snap.headerFires is the header count. snap.factoryJob stays readable while a vehicle is on the pad.

Out of scope

  • A second or third Raptor, a header tank, and flaps as a part on the sheet. The belly is a pose, not a new row.
  • A 12.5 km chip. SN8 flew that height. This row has one high hop.
  • Lighting the engine for the flip. The real flights did. This watch does not.
  • Counting a hop inside fireDays. The header matches the pad row, and the pad row does not count hops today.
  • A second factory hold, a faster build, and a new goal pay. The faster build is 1.3.0-rate.
  • Sound, the tower, and the CSS split. The split stays 0.19.1-split.
  • Editing index.html in this design pass. VERSION stays 0.18.0-hop until the row ships.

The watch

The watch always plays five pieces. The count is 10 seconds. The climb is 12. The belly is 8. The flip is 3. The landing burn is 6. The flame is on for the climb and the landing burn. The belly and the flip stay dark.

Engine seconds and fuel-line seconds fall only while the flame is on. The height number follows the clock. It rises in a straight line to 10 km during the climb. It stays at 10 km for the belly and the flip. It falls to the mark during the landing burn.

The check runs at the start of the landing burn. It names every short part. Thrust under 1535 kN names the engine. Thrust over the pad limit names the pad. Pad level 1 holds 2000 kN. Fewer than 6 seconds of fuel lines names Fuel lines. Fewer than 6 seconds of engine life names the engine.

A hard landing cuts 40 off Structure and leaves it at 20 or more. It clears the cryo proof and the tanking run. The rocket needs Mega Bay. A soft landing cuts 0 off Structure. That 0 is initial balance. The soft landing keeps the tests. Land a hop pays 60 once if that goal is still open.

A hop with 0 seconds of engine life does not start. A hop with 0 seconds of fuel lines still starts, and the landing burn names Fuel lines. The day holds for the whole watch. Skip during the watch does not jump. The log says the rocket is still in flight.

The name and the seed

The test file is index.html?test. The seed is 11. SN 1 is article 1. articleN stays the build count. The SN number counts live vehicles whose names start with SN. A destroyed vehicle leaves that count. A stored Starhopper keeps its name. The loader copies the stored name.

On seed 11, SN 1 stores Structure 58, fuel lines at 40 seconds, thrust 1814 kN, and engine life 180 seconds. That is the same body Starhopper 1 stores today. The engine check calls setThrust("SN 1", 1400) after the rocket is ready, because seed 11 is born at 1814 kN.

The builder adds setLines(name, seconds) beside setThrust. It stores the seconds on the fuel lines and returns true when the vehicle exists. The fuel-line check calls setLines("SN 1", 0) before the hop.

selectHop accepts 10000 for an SN and returns false for a Starhopper. startHop does the same. Every place that stores hopMeters keeps 10000. Every place that reads it keeps 10000. Any other stored height loads as null. Save version stays 1.

GO WITH CHANGES.

Seed 11 in index.html?test at 1280 by 800 passed the birth stats, the five-part watch, the soft and hard landings, the factory card, the bay gate, and the header fire count. Three holes remain. Two of them sit under checks that only look at one log line or at the tanks.

  1. In index.html, savePayload rewinds a live hop to ready and puts the pre-hop tanks back, and it leaves the engine life and the fuel lines at the spent values. A save during the belly reloaded ready, with methane 4029 kg and oxygen 14502 kg, while the engine had fallen from 175 seconds to 163 and the fuel lines from 40 seconds to 28. The climb had already spent those 12 seconds. Copy hop.preEngine onto engineLifeS and hop.preLines onto fittingLifeS in that same rewrite.
  2. In index.html, startHop logs methane and returns before it looks at oxygen. Full level 1 tanks are 1218 kg of methane and 4386 kg of oxygen. The 10 km hop needs 1279 kg and 4604 kg. The log was only "Methane is short. This hop needs 1279 kg. Nothing is spent." Nothing was spent. Name both short tanks in that one scrub, and do not start the hop.
  3. In index.html, the engines wiki still says a hop spends 8, 14, or 22 seconds and that a short set ends the burn early. The low hops still spend 8, 14, and 22. The 10 km hop spends 18 seconds of engine life and fuel lines, and only while the flame is on. The belly and the flip spend none. A hop with 0 seconds of fuel lines still runs the whole watch, and the landing burn names Fuel lines. Rewrite those two sentences.
  4. In FUTURE_FEATURES.md, the yard paragraph still says this row does not write a name and that the player picks 25 m, 50 m, or 150 m. The same paragraph later says a new build is named SN 1 and adds 10 km. Rewrite the older sentences so a new build is SN, a stored Starhopper keeps its name and the three low hops, and an SN also hops 10 km.
