What you see

You skip the cryo proof on TA-1. Structure stays 60. You skip the tanking run. You press Static fire with full tanks and an open range. The engine lights. The fire misses. The log names Structure. Structure falls from 60 to 20. The tests clear. The rocket needs Mega Bay.

A later article leaves with Fuel lines at 0 seconds. You skip the tanking run. The engine lights. Thrust ends as the engine lights, because a line at 0 is under the 5 second hold. The log names Fuel lines. Structure falls by 40, from 100 to 60. The lines stay at 0 seconds, because a static fire still does not spend them.

A closed range does not light. A short methane tank or a short oxygen tank does not light. The rocket keeps its life. A scrub never damages hardware.

You play a few days. You reload the file. The yard, the purse, the fleet, and the day are still there. Restart asks, then starts a new game. index.html?test does not write over that save.

The fire rule

fireShorts at line 1904 lists weather, methane, oxygen, the pad, and the engine. It does not read Structure or Fuel lines. staticFire at line 2089 calls fireCheck at line 1945. A skip counts as done. proofSettled and tankSettled at lines 2143 and 2144 treat a skip as settled. That is why a skipped short can still hold.

The floors already exist. Structure needs 70. That is PROOF_STRUCT_MIN at line 671. Fuel lines need 5 seconds. That is TANKING_LINES_MIN at line 677. A new article can leave at Structure 60, Fuel lines 0 seconds, or thrust 1400 kN. Those are DEFECT_STRUCT, DEFECT_LINES_S, and DEFECT_THRUST_KN at lines 679 to 681. The engine floor stays 1500 kN. That is SF_THRUST_BAR_KN at line 728.

fireCheck keeps the shared list, then adds two shorts of its own. Add structure when stage life is under 70. Add fitting when fuel-line seconds are under 5. Those two checks stay in fireCheck. fireShorts stays the shared list. The hidden launch calls it too, and it already has its own fuel-line rule.

The order on the fire is weather, methane, oxygen, Structure, Fuel lines, the pad, then the engine. The log names every short part, not only the first.

isScrub at line 1915 stays weather, methane, and oxygen. Structure and Fuel lines are not scrubs. A short pad and a short engine are not scrubs. Those four can light.

A lit miss uses the rule that already exists. afterFireLives at line 1937 takes 40 off Structure. That 40 is SF_MISS_CONDITION at line 729. It takes 40 once, even when two parts are short. The engine loses 5 seconds, unless the engine itself is short. A short engine still sets the seconds to 0. Fuel-line seconds do not change. Scrap is still under 20. That is SCRAP_FLOOR at line 732. Structure 60 becomes 20, and the rocket stays. Structure 100 becomes 60.

The debrief names the part the player knows. Structure reads Structure. Fuel lines read Fuel lines. shortReason at line 2015 has no Structure line today. The fallback says the engine is short. Add a Structure line so a structure miss cannot wear the engine's name. The fuel-line phrase is already there.

A fire that lights marks Structure and Fuel lines as tested, because the fire read them. It still marks the engine tested, as it does today. The sheet can then show 60, or 0 seconds, instead of hiding them behind "not tested". worstPart at line 1240 skips an untested part. That is why a clean engine can read 97 percent while Structure 60 stays hidden. After this row, a short that the fire read is on the sheet.

A lit miss still clears the cryo proof, the tanking run, and both skips. A clean fire still keeps them. applySfOutcome at line 2310 already does that. The first lit fire still pays 60. The first clean fire still pays 80. Those are GOAL_PAY at lines 636 and 639. A miss still loads 609 kg of methane and 2193 kg of liquid oxygen. A scrub still loads nothing.

Short Fuel lines keep the pad rule. The engine lights. Thrust ends at the Fuel lines seconds, capped at the 5 second hold. A line at 0 cuts as the engine lights. The hidden launch asks for 169 seconds, FITTING_BURN_S at line 768. Thrust ends at 34 seconds, FITTING_ABORT_S at line 770. startFly at line 2441 sets that cut. Those two numbers stay on the launch. The static fire does not copy them. For a fuel-line miss, the anomaly label reads Fuel lines, and the log says thrust ends early. A Fuel-lines miss takes 40 Structure because every lit miss does, per decision 5.

A failed cryo proof and a failed tanking run stay what they are. They name the part and damage nothing. This row does not change their prices, their days, or their floors.

The bay buttons

Tiago locked this on 2026-11-12. Until the pad has checked a part, the fix button for that part stays off. You cannot click Structure in Mega Bay and set it to 100% before the cryo proof. The same goes for the fuel lines and the engine. Skip a test and that fix button stays off. The static fire then shows the weak part the hard way. After that, the bay can fix it.

The cryo proof checks Structure. Until that check has finished on this rocket, "Refurb the structure" cannot start. The tanking run checks the fuel lines. Until that check has finished, "Inspect the fuel lines" cannot start. The static fire checks the engine. Until a static fire has lit on this rocket, "Refurb the engine" cannot start.

A failed cryo proof has still checked Structure, so that one button can start. A failed tanking run has still checked the fuel lines. A static fire that lights has checked Structure, the fuel lines, and the engine. A closed range or a short tank does not light, damages nothing, and does not count as the check. The days, the steel, and the words on the three jobs stay as they are. The bay still runs one job at a time.

The save

The live file has no localStorage and no sessionStorage. restartGame at line 5204 reloads the page, and the yard is gone. This row writes one save in the browser.

The key is starbase-save. The version key inside the file is version, and the value is 1. A file with any other version stays on disk and does not load. The game starts empty and logs that this save is from another version. A file that will not parse stays on disk too. The game logs that the save could not be read, then starts empty. The game does not delete a file it does not understand.

The label sits next to the version so a person can see the row. seed is null. 0.14.2-seed fills it later. This row does not roll defects or weather from a seed. Article n still takes part (n - 1) modulo 3. The sky stays the 10-day table.

The sample above is the shape, not a real yard. A vehicle keeps the fields finishFactoryJob already writes at line 1544, plus fires, needsDetank, and the test flags. A building keeps id, type, gx, gy, and level. A rollout keeps the plain fields a road roll, a raise, or a lower already uses. The log keeps the message and the kind, up to the 45 lines log() already stores at line 1093.

The save writes the fields the live game reads. It does not write the dormant kerosene stock. The camera, the open sheet, and the drag are not saved. The map opens the way a new game opens. nextId must stay above every saved id. If it is missing, set it to one more than the highest id in the file.

Autosave runs at the end of dayTick at line 1408, after the jobs for that day resolve. The saved day is the new day. If a fill, a fire, or a flight is on the clock, the save waits for the next day tick after that motion is gone. A road roll, a raise, and a lower can sit in the file. On load, those motions resume. A motion whose vehicle is missing is dropped. The day boundary is enough for a buy or a roll. The game never saves on a buy or a roll.

The game also saves on pagehide, and only when no fill, fire, or flight is running. A reload during one of those motions keeps the last save. A reload while the pad is idle writes the open day first, then the page continues that yard.

Reload, Restart, and test

A normal open looks for starbase-save. If the version is 1, the game continues that yard and does not add the three intro lines. If the key is missing, the game starts as it does today. The purse is 150. Steel is 0. The tanks and the yard are empty. Day 1 has no save yet. The first autosave lands when day 1 becomes day 2, and only if no fill, fire, or flight is in the way.

Restart still opens the card at line 598. The words stay "Start a new game? The current yard is lost." Cancel closes the card and leaves the save. Start over removes starbase-save, then reloads. The new page finds no save and starts empty.

?test is the flag at line 613. README already says that start is not saved. This row keeps that promise. A test page does not read starbase-save and does not write it. Autosave does not run. Restart on a test page reloads and skips the confirm, as it does today, and it does not delete starbase-save.

The hook saveNow writes a different key, starbase-save-test, and returns true. It never writes the real key. loadSave reads the test key only. If that key is missing, it returns false and leaves the test yard. Opening the test page does not call either hook. A player who opens ?test beside a real game does not lose the real game.
