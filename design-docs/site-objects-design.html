On the map now

These objects are in index.html at 0.2.1-field. Each one is a single thing. It has no hall, crane, berth, trench, or tank of its own. The parts further down this page are the plan. The game does not create them yet.

Land

You buy a parcel before you place a building on it. An owned parcel is a copper line, #8a3e24, with a pale stake at each corner. The parcel still for sale is a dashed copper line.

The road

The road is not a thing you buy, and it is not a part of one building. Once two of the three buildings are placed, the map strokes one sand road from the east edge of one to the west edge of the next, bowing north. The pale stroke is 16 m wide. The darker stroke inside it is 9 m wide. A branch that ends at each hall is the later road, in the list below. It is not this stroke.

The three buildings

You place one of each. The box is the whole object. Level starts at 1.

The vehicle

Falcon 1 is its own object. It costs 28 steel and takes 3 days. A static fire draws 8 propellant. It is built at the factory, housed in the bay, rolled along the road, and fired on the pad. Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, and Starship are locked names. They are not on the map. Merlin is a number on the factory card, not a separate object. The rocket sheet lists the Merlin, the fuel fitting, and the stage. Each one has a life and its own refurb. The sheet paints each part on the rocket as a percent. The big number is the lowest part. A static fire does not spend the fuel fitting. Launch needs 169 seconds on that part. A hard flight does not cut stage life until 0.13.0-recovery.

The rule

A main building is one object you buy. A part is also one object. It has its own id, its own place on that main, and its own job. It is not a caption painted near the building.

The part stores a seat on its parent: which side, and how far from the parent’s origin. The map draws the part from that seat. Zooming in shows the part. Zooming out shows the parent. The names do not all stay on screen.

Selecting a part opens that part. Selecting empty ground on the parent opens the parent. The left column lists the mains. It does not list every part.

Born with the main

When the main is placed, its parts are created with it. You do not buy the deluge plate as a separate plot. You buy the pad. The plate, the pumps, the trench, and the water tank arrive as parts of that pad.

A part can be quiet. Quiet means it is real and you can select it, and it does not change the fire yet. The version that needs it turns the job on. Until then the panel says what it will do, in one sentence.

A second pad is not a part of the first pad. Massey’s is not a part of the factory. A separate site is a separate main, and it waits for its own row.

Infrastructure

Placing a main also places the works it needs. You do not buy the road, the power plant, or the water tank as their own plots. The game picks every seat. The layout is fixed, so the map can use one picture. The look of each building can be drawn later. This list is the plan. It is not in the game yet. The road in the section above is the one the map draws today.

A road is one of those works. It is a branch from the site road to that main, and it ends at the building. It does not stop in the water. The field lab has a test of that road. The test is not this list.

When you placeThe game also places
StarfactoryHall, crane, stack stand, a power plant, and a road to the hall.
Mega BayHall, berth, service crane, and a road to the hall.
Orbital padMount, flame trench, deluge plate, deluge pumps, water tank, and a road to the pad.

The first main on a site lays the shared road. Each later main adds its own branch. A pad upgrade still adds only its own parts, the tank farm or the tower. It does not lay a second road.

What you see

  • Far: the main’s body and its name.
  • Near: the parts, each with a body. The name shows for the part under the pointer, and for the one you selected.
  • The buy column: the main, its price, and its area. Parts stay off that list.

It is not a list of every Starbase name drawn at once. It is not a second copy of the factory floating beside the sprite. The live screen that does that is the mistake this draft replaces.

Orbital pad

You buy one pad. It starts as the mount and the water system. Two upgrades add the rest. You can buy either one, or both. They are not levels of the same ladder. Pad 2 is a different main, and it gets the same choices.

Later mains

  • Pad 2. Its own mount, tower, and tanks. Same part list as the first pad. Not a part of pad 1.
  • High bay, second mega bay, Sanchez. Each is its own hall on the build site. Not rooms inside Starfactory.
  • Massey’s. Its own site inland. Parts: ship cryo stand, booster cryo stand, static-fire stand, methane farm. None of those ride along with the factory.
